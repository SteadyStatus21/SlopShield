Welcome to SlopShield

SlopShield warns you about AI-generated videos and pictures while you scroll Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and other sites. Everything is checked on this computer; nothing you watch is sent anywhere.

  1. 01

    Install the SlopShield helper Recommended

    The helper is a small app that uses your computer's graphics chip to check videos about 3× faster, with less lag and battery use. Without it, SlopShield still works inside the browser, just more slowly.

    Download the helper (Windows) ↓
    1. Open the downloaded ZIP file and choose Extract all.
    2. In the extracted folder, open engine and double-click setup_engine.bat. The first run installs everything it needs (about 2 minutes). If it asks for Python, install it from python.org ↗ with "Add python.exe to PATH" ticked, then run it again.
    3. Leave the helper window open while you browse. Next time, double-click start_engine.bat.
